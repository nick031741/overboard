When submitting a request form on the website, the following personal data may be collected:

Personal data is processed based on:

4. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • until the project or negotiations are completed;

After the purpose has been achieved, data is deleted or anonymised.

5. Data Sharing

Personal data is not shared with third parties, except where required by applicable law.

This website does not use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Yandex.Metrica) that would transmit user data to external parties.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • request information about how your personal data is being processed;
  • request correction, blocking, or deletion of your data if it is incomplete, outdated, or unlawfully processed;
  • withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

To withdraw consent or exercise any of the above rights, please contact: nickpremium1@proton.me

7. Data Security

The Operator takes reasonable organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Cookies

This website may use cookies to ensure proper functionality. Cookies do not contain personal data and are used for technical purposes only. You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

The Operator reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always available at overboardstudio.com/en/privacy.html.
