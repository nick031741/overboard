Our Projects

1️⃣ Telegram bot for a restaurant or coffee shop

The bot helps restaurants handle table reservations, display the latest menu, and communicate with guests — without phone calls, managers, or endless messages.

  • 📅 Online table reservations — Guests choose the date, time, and number of people. The reservation is instantly recorded and the administrator receives a notification.
  • 📋 Up-to-date menu — The bot sends the menu with a single tap, making it easy for guests to browse without extra links.
  • 🎁 Promotions and special offers — Guests can view current promotions such as happy hours, birthday discounts, or daily specials.
  • 🔁 Repeat visits and takeaway orders — The bot stores reservations, can send visit reminders, suggest repeat bookings, collect feedback, or help place takeaway orders.
📊 Results:
  • Reduced workload for staff
  • 24/7 reservations and order processing
  • Fewer missed booking requests
  • A convenient communication channel with guests on Telegram

2️⃣ Telegram bot for a fitness center

The bot helps fitness clubs introduce their services to new clients, display the latest class schedule, and answer common questions automatically — without the need for administrators.

  • ℹ️ Club information — The bot introduces new users to the fitness club, explaining the available zones, equipment, and what makes the club unique.
  • 📅 Class schedule — Users can quickly check the latest group class schedule and choose a convenient time to visit the gym.
  • 💪 List of coaches — Users can browse the list of trainers, learn about their specialization and experience, and choose the right trainer for their goals.
  • 💳 Membership plans and FAQs — The bot clearly presents membership options and answers common questions.
📊 Results:
  • Reduced workload for staff
  • 24/7 access to information and schedules
  • Fewer repetitive questions and a convenient client communication channel on Telegram

3️⃣ Telegram bot for events

The bot helps event organizers automate participant registration, send reminders, and provide access links — without manual work or third-party tools.

  • 📝 Participant registration — The bot collects user data step by step, such as name, age, or any required details. Simple and intuitive — no external forms or links needed.
  • ⏰ Automated reminders and access links — Users receive a "Join" button at the right time, minimizing no-shows and missed events.
  • 🎓 Perfect for online courses and events — A flexible solution for online schools, training programs, webinars, HR events, and live sessions.
📊 Results:
  • Fully automated participant registration
  • Less manual work for organizers
  • Higher attendance rates and a smooth joining experience

4️⃣ AI Telegram bot for an online school

The bot helps online schools engage with potential students, provide product consultations, and guide users toward conversion — without the need for sales managers.

  • 💬 Natural, human-like conversations — The AI understands context and user intent, responds like a real consultant, and adapts to the flow of the conversation.
  • ℹ️ Product information on demand — Automatically provides up-to-date pricing, key benefits, and bonuses.
  • 🧩 Handles objections effectively — Identifies user concerns and responds with personalized arguments that highlight the value of the offer.
  • 🎯 Drives users to conversion — Naturally guides the conversation toward booking a free trial lesson or consultation.
📊 Results:
  • 24/7 automated consultations
  • Reduced workload for sales managers
  • More bookings for free lessons and higher conversion rates
